08:50 · Out the door

It works on your computer. You watch from your phone.

Replies stream in word by word; what it read, changed and ran updates live, with a diff for every file. When it needs permission or a choice from you, the request comes to your phone: one tap.

09:20 · On the train

One tap on the Lock Screen. Approved.

A Live Activity keeps the agent's progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; when it asks for permission, the card carries Deny and Allow. On Apple Watch you can follow sessions, answer requests and send a short reply.

Live Activities need iOS 16.2 or later, buttons on the card iOS 17. The watch app needs watchOS 10 or later.

12:40 · Lunch

It is busy. Cut in anyway.

A message sent mid-turn waits in a queue: reorder it, take it back to rewrite, or send it now, straight into Codex's or Claude's running turn. A turn running on the computer can be stopped from the phone too.

Wherever the session was opened: linkshell claude or linkshell codex in a terminal, a plain claude or codex, or their desktop apps.

15:10 · Between meetings

Your Mac's desktop. In your pocket.

When an agent finishes, open the actual desktop on your phone: check a page, touch the editor, dismiss a dialog. Turn it sideways, go full screen, pinch to zoom; switch between trackpad and direct touch, and send long text or your clipboard across.

Up to 3840 pixels wide and 120 Hz on iOS; real results depend on devices and network. Full control needs macOS 13 or later.

16:30 · Still in the meeting

Let Codex do the clicking.

From your phone, ask Codex to use the browser and desktop apps on your computer, and watch it work in a small window above the conversation. Keep talking while it works; drag the window aside, enlarge it or tuck it away. Codex on macOS for now. How to use it.

18:30 · Ride home

Unfold iPhone Duo. Chat beside the changes.

Open it up and the conversation sits beside the changes or a preview. Fold it and the layout rearranges around the screen, without leaving the session. A regular iPhone gets one column with the same controls.

21:30 · Living room

Fusion Mode brings another Mac's apps over one at a time. They show up in this Mac's Dock and ⌘Tab, marked bottom right with the computer they come from, each in its own windows among your local apps.

More you can do from the phone.

A real terminal on your computer
Run a dev server, tail logs or use git, with a Ctrl / Esc / Tab / arrow-key bar. It keeps running when you close the app.
Open localhost on your phone
Your computer's localhost:3000 right on the phone, hot reload included: no open ports, no shared Wi-Fi.
Forks and worktrees
Fork a new session from any reply to try another direction, in a new git worktree if you like, away from what you are working on.
Side conversations
Ask something without interrupting the turn under way, the agent's own way: Codex's /side, Claude's and Grok's /btw. Nothing of it enters the session.
Sub-agents and Workflows
Follow Claude Workflows by phase and open each Agent’s conversation and tool results. Background runs stay above the composer; the header lists all Workflows and sub-agents.
Slash commands and skills
Type / for the agent's commands and your skills. /compact and /review work for Codex too.
A full workspace on the Mac
The Mac app continues conversations, handles approvals and opens terminals and remote desktops, on this Mac or another computer. It also shows token usage by agent, launches at login and comes in light and dark.
Files and tidy sessions
Browse the project's files and send photos and files from your phone to the agent. Rename, archive or delete sessions; claude --resume shows the new name.

Three steps to your computer.

On Mac or Windows, download the desktop app. On Linux, or if you prefer the command line, install the CLI (Node.js 22.13 or newer).

1

Install

The desktop app brings its own CLI, Host and Node and updates them together. On a Mac (macOS 13 or later), turn on Screen Recording and Accessibility in its Settings.

Mac · Apple siliconMac · IntelWindows

$ npm i -g linkshell-cli
$ linkshell setup
2

Connect your phone

Official gateway: sign in with the same account on the computer and in the app, and the computer appears; new accounts get a 7-day free trial, no card needed. Your own gateway: pair once with a QR code.

$ linkshell login
# or your own gateway
$ linkshell host --gateway wss://gw.you
$ linkshell pair
3

Get going

Start a session on the phone, or launch from your terminal like this and hand it over any time. The docs have the details.

$ linkshell claude
$ linkshell codex
