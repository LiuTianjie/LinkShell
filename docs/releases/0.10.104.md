# LinkShell Desktop 0.10.104

- Attachments selected in the desktop or browser composer reach the agent, including new sessions.
- Windows video survives brief transport drops; the remote-screen fallback copy matches the active route.
- Desktop membership pages support LinkShell monthly/annual purchases and the all-products annual bundle. Purchase links open iTool with the intended product selected, and the page no longer infers access to other products from a monthly or yearly label.

macOS downloads are signed and notarized. Windows x64 downloads are unsigned; Windows may show an unknown-publisher or SmartScreen prompt.
